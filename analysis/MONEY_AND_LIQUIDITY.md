# Monetary Amounts and Liquidity Data

Yes. The supplied datasets contain market prices, trading volumes and issuer
financial-statement amounts. They do **not** contain client account balances,
assets under management, shares outstanding or market capitalisation.

## Available monetary fields

| Dataset | Fields | Unit | Use in the analysis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily and intraday prices | `open`, `high`, `low`, `close` | USD per share | Returns, volatility and drawdown calculations |
| Daily and intraday prices | `volume` | Shares / units traded | Liquidity assessment; `close × volume` produces estimated USD traded value |
| Fundamentals | `revenue_usd_m`, `ebitda_usd_m`, `net_income_usd_m`, `debt_usd_m`, `cash_usd_m` | USD millions | Growth, margins, net debt and leverage analysis |
| Macro | `usd_cop`, `usd_pen`, `usd_mxn`, `usd_clp` | Local-currency units per USD | Translation of USD portfolio performance into a client’s base currency |
| Client profiles / twist | Drawdown limits and a 30% liquidity requirement | Percentages only | Portfolio suitability and cash-sleeve design |

All asset prices in the reference universe are USD-denominated.  Price and
volume fields should be interpreted as market data, not as an investor's
position value.

## Market liquidity amounts

The analysis calculates average daily traded value as:

`average daily traded value (USD) = close price (USD/share) × volume (shares)`

Across the recent daily-price window, average daily traded value ranges from
approximately **$52m to $175m** by asset. This supports the liquidity scenario
in the twist analysis, while retaining an important caveat: daily and intraday
price feeds disagree in the overlap period, so liquidity estimates must use one
source at a time and must not be combined.

## What cannot be quantified from the supplied data

- **Actual client money / portfolio size:** no account balance, AUM or position
  size appears in `05_client_profiles.csv`.
- **Market capitalisation and valuation multiples:** shares outstanding are
  absent, so market cap, P/E and EV/EBITDA cannot be calculated.
- **Absolute value of the 30% withdrawal:** `dataset_twist.csv` gives only a
  percentage. The `$100m` portfolio and `$30m` redemption in
  `10_twist_analysis.py` are explicitly illustrative scenario assumptions, not
  source-data facts.
